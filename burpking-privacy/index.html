Burp King — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android app Burp King (package com.zaryopgames.burpking) distributed on Google Play.
Effective 25 October 2026 · Last updated 25 October 2026

The short version. Burp King has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not collect your name, e-mail, photos, contacts, microphone or location, and we run no analytics. Your progress is saved in one file on your own device and never leaves it. Burp King is made for players aged 13 and over. It shows ads from Google AdMob, limited to a Teen content rating; where the law allows, Google may personalize them, and where the law requires it, you are asked first and can change your answer at any time.

1.Who we are

Burp King is developed and published by The Zaryop Games ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains how the Burp King mobile game handles information. It covers the app itself — not any other website or service that happens to link here.

If you have any question about this policy, write to keskinl@gmail.com.

2.What data is collected

We collect nothing about you. Burp King has no accounts, no sign-up, no login and no user profiles. We do not ask for, receive, or store:

  • your name, e-mail address, phone number or postal address;
  • your contacts, photos, files, calendar or SMS;
  • your microphone, camera or location;
  • analytics, crash telemetry, behavioural events or advertising profiles.

We operate no servers that receive data from the game, and we have no database of players. There is nothing about you for us to sell, share or lose — and we do not sell or share any data with anyone.

Google, as our advertising and billing provider, does receive limited technical information when it serves an ad or processes a purchase. That is described in section 5 and section 6. We never receive it.

3.Data stored on your device

Your points, level, unlocked skins, daily challenge progress, streaks and settings are saved in a single file on your own device, in the app's private storage. That file is not readable by other apps, is never uploaded anywhere, and is not backed up to any service we control.

Uninstalling Burp King deletes that file — and your progress with it. There is no cloud save and no way to restore it.

4.Internet connection

Burp King needs an active internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data) to play. When you are offline, the game shows a warning and will not start a round.

To check that the device is really online, the game asks a Google connectivity address for an empty reply. That request contains no personal data and no identifiers — it only establishes whether the network is reachable.

5.Advertising (Google AdMob)

Burp King shows ads supplied by Google AdMob:

  • a short full-screen (interstitial) ad after every second completed round;
  • optional rewarded ads you choose to watch to earn Snack Tickets or a Daily Boost — nothing is taken away if you skip them.

There are no banner ads anywhere in the game.

How our ad requests are configured

Every ad request Burp King makes is sent with:

  • Maximum ad content rating "T" — ads suitable for teens and older;
  • Personalized ads where permitted — Google may choose ads based on your device's advertising identifier and your activity in other apps and websites;
  • Consent first where the law requires it — in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) asks you before any ad is requested, and the game respects your answer.

What Google receives

To show, measure and (where allowed) personalize ads, and to detect and prevent ad fraud, Google's advertising SDK collects and receives:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of the device;
  • interactions such as app launches, taps and ad video views;
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance;
  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. It goes to Google, not to us — we never receive it.

Your choices

  • Where consent applies, the PRIVACY button on the game's home screen opens Google's form again so you can change your answer at any time.
  • On Android you can turn off personalized ads, or reset or delete your advertising ID, in Settings → Google → Ads.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies: Google Advertising and Google Privacy Policy.

6.In-app purchases

Burp King contains exactly one purchase: Remove Ads, a one-time, non-consumable item that permanently turns off the full-screen interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads keep working afterwards, so nothing you have unlocked becomes unreachable.

The transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see, receive or store your card number, billing address or any payment details. We only receive from Google Play the confirmation that the item was purchased, so the app can honour it.

For parents: please make sure a child asks the owner of the device before buying anything. You can require a password or biometric confirmation for every purchase in Google Play → Settings → Authentication → Require authentication for purchases. Refunds are handled by Google Play, not by us.

7.Children's privacy

Burp King is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Its ads are not requested as child-directed, which is why the game should not be handed to a younger child.

  • we collect no personal information at all, from anyone, of any age;
  • there is no chat, no messaging, no user-generated content, no social features, no friend lists and no way for a player to be contacted inside the game;
  • there are no external links or web views to browse away into, other than the ads described above;
  • there are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play mid-round.

In line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU GDPR (including Article 8 on children's consent), we do not knowingly collect, use or disclose personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Because we collect nothing, there is nothing for a parent to review or ask us to erase from our systems. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information to us, please contact keskinl@gmail.com and we will investigate and delete anything found.

To remove everything the game has stored about a child's play: uninstall Burp King. That deletes the on-device save file completely. No copy exists anywhere else.

9.Retention and deletion

We retain no player data, so there is no retention period on our side. Game progress lives only on your device and is kept until you delete the app or clear its storage. Data held by Google in connection with ads or purchases is retained according to Google's own policies, linked above.

10.Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (Europe), UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA (California) and Türkiye's KVKK give you rights to access, correct, delete, port or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct or erase on request — but you are welcome to write to us at keskinl@gmail.com and we will answer.

For data that Google may process as our advertising and billing provider, exercise your rights through your Google Account privacy controls at myaccount.google.com/privacy.

On Android you can also limit ad tracking at any time via Settings → Google → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID.

11.Security

The game's save file is written to the app's private storage, which the Android operating system isolates from other apps. All network traffic the game makes uses HTTPS. Since we hold no personal data on any server, there is no account to be breached and no credentials to be stolen from us.

12.Policy updates

We may update this policy — for example if we add a feature that changes what the game does. When that happens we will:

  • publish the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top;
  • for any change that materially affects your privacy, show the policy again inside the game and ask you to accept the new version before you continue playing.

Continuing to play after an update means you accept the current version. If you do not accept it, you can decline in the game and uninstall the app.
